Formulas
  • DIO = Inventory ÷ COGS × days in year
  • DSO = Accounts receivable ÷ Revenue × days in year (all sales on credit)
  • DPO = Accounts payable ÷ COGS × days in year (purchases equal COGS)
  • CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO
